Privacy Policy

Version of 24 November 2026

This policy explains what data the LeDor mobile app and the kalendar.bothub.chat website process, why, for how long it is kept and how you can control it.

1. Who is responsible

LeDor is a non-commercial open-source project. The data controller is the app’s author, a private individual (Gershon). Contact: the “More → Send feedback” form in the app or the author’s Telegram channel.

2. In short

  • No sign-up, ads, analytics or trackers.
  • We do not sell data or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.
  • The app does not request location, contacts, photos or other permissions it does not need.

3. What is stored only on your phone

  • your personal list of events (names, dates, notes);
  • settings, the city chosen for sunset times, and the language;
  • PINs of connected shared lists.

This data never leaves your phone unless you move events to a shared list.

4. What is stored on the server

Shared lists — only if you create or connect a shared list: the list name, events (name or title, type, dates, note), technical timestamps and versions. The regular PIN is stored so editors can share the list; the Master PIN only as an irreversible hash. Purpose: syncing the list between members.

Feedback — if you send feedback: its text and, only if you provided or allowed them, a contact for a reply and technical details (app version, phone model, Android version, language). Events and PINs are never included. Purpose: replying to you and improving the app. Feedback is forwarded to the author via Telegram.

Technical data — every request to the server involves your IP address. To protect against PIN guessing, only its irreversible hash is stored.

Data is stored on the developer’s server; connections are encrypted (HTTPS).

5. How long data is kept

  • a shared list and its events — until the list is deleted;
  • feedback — no longer than 2 years;
  • IP address hash — no longer than one day;
  • database backups — 14 days.

6. Legal bases

Shared lists and syncing — providing what you ask for by creating or connecting a list. Feedback — your consent, given by sending it. Protection against PIN guessing — our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

7. Who can see a shared list

Anyone who knows its name and PIN. You decide whom to give the PIN to and are responsible for the consent of the people whose dates you add.

8. Who receives data

No one, except where required by law. The website uses the Cloudflare network, which technically processes visitors’ IP addresses. Feedback is delivered to the author via Telegram.

9. Your rights and deleting data

You can ask what data about you is stored, correct it or delete it.

  • An entire shared list: “Lists” → the list → “Delete the list from the cloud” → Master PIN. The list is permanently deleted for all members.
  • A single event: open the event → “Delete”. It disappears for everyone after syncing.
  • Disconnect a list from your phone: “Lists” → the list → “Disconnect”.
  • Everything on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.
  • Feedback, a lost Master PIN, other requests: write via “Send feedback” in the app with the list name and a contact for a reply. We reply within 30 days.

If you are in the EU, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

10. Android backups

App data may be included in your phone’s backup in your Google account — this is controlled by your phone settings.

11. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

12. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. We will announce significant changes in the app.
