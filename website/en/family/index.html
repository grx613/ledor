Shared family calendar

You and your spouse (or the whole family) want to see the same dates: whoever adds a birthday, it shows up for both of you, and the reminder comes to both phones. That is what a LeDor shared list is for. Only people you give its name and code can see it.

  1. 1

    Open “Lists”

    On the calendar or in “Events”, tap “Lists” at the top. You will see your “Personal” list (it lives only on this phone) and the “Create” and “Connect” buttons.

  2. 2

    Create a shared list

    Tap “Create”. Choose a name — say, “My Family” — and two codes: a read PIN (4–8 digits, for viewing only) and a master PIN (6–12 digits, for adding and editing). If your “Personal” list already has dates, tick “Move events from the personal list”.

  3. 3

    Share it with your family

    Open the new list. So that your spouse can add dates too, turn on “Share editing rights” and tap WhatsApp or Telegram. For relatives who only need to see the dates, send the invitation without that switch.

  4. 4

    They connect the list

    They install LeDor, open “Lists” → “Connect” and enter the list name and code from the message. Nothing else: no sign-up, no email.

  5. 5

    Done — a shared calendar

    Now whatever any of you adds, everyone sees, and reminders come to every phone. New events go to the shared list by default. A colored tag shows which list an event belongs to.

Frequently asked questions

Who else can see our list?

No one. A list opens only with its name and code. The server does not allow guessing: after a few wrong tries, attempts are limited.

What is the difference between the two codes?

The read PIN lets you see the dates and get reminders. The master PIN also lets you add, edit and delete. Give the master PIN only to people you trust.

On first launch the app asked about “importing a list”. What file do I need?

None. It is the same as “Connect”: if a relative has already created a shared list, enter its name and code. If not, tap “No” and create your own as described above.

What if I do not want to share anything?

Use the “Personal” list: it is stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere.

Can I have several lists?

Yes: “Family”, “Community”, “Friends” — each with its own color and codes. Notifications can be turned on or off for each list.
