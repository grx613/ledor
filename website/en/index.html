A shared list: the same dates for the whole family

The heart of LeDor is a list kept by the whole family or community. No accounts, emails or passwords.

  1. 1

    Create a list

    For example, “Cohen family”. The app creates two PINs: one for viewing and one for editing.

  2. 2

    Send the PIN to your family

    One tap in WhatsApp or Telegram. They install LeDor and enter the list name and PIN.

  3. 3

    The dates appear for everyone

    Add a grandchild’s birthday — grandma and uncle get it too, with reminders by their own settings.

  • No sign-up. No email, phone number or password — just the list name and PIN.
  • Two access levels. The regular PIN lets you view. The Master PIN lets you add and edit.
  • Works offline. Changes are sent once you are back online.
  • Several lists at once. Family, community, friends — each with its own color and notifications.

Changes arrive when someone opens the app or pulls the list down, and once a day in the background.

Frequently asked questions

How do I share a list with my family?

Create a shared list under “Lists” and tap Telegram or WhatsApp — your family gets a message with the list name, PIN and a link to the app.

Do I need to sign up? Do you collect my data?

There is no sign-up. The app has no ads, analytics or trackers. The personal list stays on your phone; a shared list is stored on our server for its members only.

What is the difference between the PIN and the Master PIN?

The regular PIN lets people view dates and get reminders. The Master PIN lets them add, change and delete events. Share it only with people you trust.

Can I celebrate a birthday by both calendars?

Yes. Choose “By both calendars” — there will be two dates a year, and LeDor reminds you of each. The year of birth is needed to convert the date.

Does it account for sunset?

Yes. When entering a date you can mark “after sunset” — the Hebrew date is then the next one. The evening reminder comes a chosen time before sunset in your city.

How is a yahrzeit calculated in a leap year?

For someone who passed away in Adar of a regular year, Ashkenazi custom observes it in Adar I and Sephardi custom in Adar II; you choose the custom in settings. The rules for the 30th of Cheshvan and Kislev are applied too.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. The internet is needed only for shared lists; changes are sent once you are back online.

How much battery does the app use?

Practically none. There are no background services, no location and no permanent connection. The phone wakes LeDor for a split second only at the moment of a reminder — the evening before and on the day — and, if you use a shared list, once a day to sync, when the system finds it convenient.

Which languages does LeDor support?

English, עברית, Русский, ייִדיש, Français, Español. In Hebrew and Yiddish the interface is right-to-left and Hebrew dates are written in letters.

Can I move my events to a shared list?

Yes. When creating a list, check “Move events from the personal list”, or open the personal list and choose “Move events to a cloud list”.

Is there a dark theme?

Yes: light, dark or system — under “Settings → Appearance”.

Is there an iPhone version?

Coming soon. LeDor is currently available for Android.

How much does the app cost?

It is free, with no ads or paid content. The project is non-commercial and open source.
